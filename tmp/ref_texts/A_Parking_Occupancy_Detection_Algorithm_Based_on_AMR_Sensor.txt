A Parking Occupancy Detection Algorithm
Based on AMR Sensor
Zusheng Zhang, Ming Tao, and Huaqiang Yuan
Abstract— Recently, with the explosive increase of automobiles
in cities, parking problems are serious and even worsen in many
cities. This paper proposes a new algorithm for parking occu-
pancy detection based on the use of anisotropic magnetoresistive
sensors. Parking occupancy detection is abstracted as binary
pattern recognition problem. According to the status of the park-
ing space, the recognition result contains two categories: 1) vacant
and 2) occupied. The feature extraction method of the parking
magnetic signal is proposed. In addition, the classiﬁcation criteria
are derived based on the distance discriminate analysis method.
Eighty-two sensor nodes are deployed on the roadside parking
spaces. By running the system for six months, we observed that
the accuracy rate of the proposed parking occupancy detection
algorithm is better than 98%.
Index Terms— AMR sensor, wireless sensor networks, parking
occupancy detection.
I. I NTRODUCTION
A
T PRESENT, the sensors used in the trafﬁc informa-
tion acquisition include the following types: inductive
loop detector [1], image (camera) sensor [2], [3], acoustic
sensor [4], [5], infrared sensor [6], ultrasonic sensor [7],
etc. The image sensor acquires an abundance of information,
but it is vulnerable to bad weather and nighttime operation.
The acoustic sensor and infrared sensor are vulnerable to
noise in deployed environments. Magnetic sensors based on
magneto resistors have recently been proposed for vehicle
detection [8]–[11] because they are quite sensitive, small and
more immune to environmental factors such as rain, wind,
snow or fog than sensing systems based on video cameras,
ultrasound or infrared radiation.

B. Static Trafﬁc Information Acquisition
Technologies normally employed within indoor car parks,
such as ultrasonic sensors [19], could not be used outdoor
because every space would need to be wired. Installing cables
and wires along the roadside for data transmission and energy
supply is very difﬁcult and challenging. So a completely wire-
less solution was adopted. Wireless AMR sensor networks [20]
have lots of potential toward providing an ideal solution for
roadside parking service, such as their low power, small size,
low cost, and high accuracy.
The use of AMR sensors for detection of vehicle in parking
space has been previously studied. In [21], S. Lee discussed the
effect of detecting vehicles by comparing the acoustic, visual
light, infrared, temperature, ultrasonic, and AMR sensors.
Their experiments veriﬁed th at ultrasonic and AMR sensors
have better accuracy and reliability in the parking space.
Threshold detection algorithms are usually to measure the
geomagnetic ﬁeld change of z-axis [22], [23]. If this change
in frequency exceeds a threshold, the sensor will indicate
the detection of a vehicle. But a single threshold cannot
apply to all situations and often leads to a high false alarm
rate. In order to solve this problem, an adaptive threshold

problems and propose a more accurate detection algorithm.

(a) By Analysis of the characteristics of the disturbance geo-
magnetic signal, the parking vehicle detection algorithm

for the interference signal.
(b) Vehicle leaving can be regarded as an inverse process of
its entering. Choosing the signal of the entering phase as
the referential signal, leaving detection is to computing
the similarity between the two phases.

II. P
ARKING OCCUPANCY DETECTION ALGORITHM
According to the status of the parking space, parking
occupancy detection is a binary recognition problem (two
cases: vacant and occupied). The key issue is to train the
classiﬁcation criteria from the magnetic signal. Solve the prob-
lem involve following steps: Firstly, analysis characteristics of
the magnetic signal and computing the features variable of
ﬂuctuate signal. Secondly, classiﬁcation rules are trained using
the distance discriminate method, and the training data are pre-
classiﬁed examples (class label is known for each example).
Finally, the parking detection algorithm is designed based on
classiﬁcation criteria.

the values of x, y, z are the background’s magnetic ﬁelds. Then

leaving the parking space, and also has a ﬂuctuation signature.
Finally, the parking space is vacant.

B. Classiﬁcation Criteria
1) Parking Detection: In our experiments, we found that
some of the vehicles disturb the geomagnetic ﬁeld greater.
They often bring interference to the sensors at the adjacent
parking spaces. For simplicity, we deﬁne two kinds of signals:
1) The parking signal which is perceived by the sensor node
deployed on the vehicle parked space. 2) The interference
signal which is perceived by the sensors at the neighbor
parking spaces. The parking signa l and the interference signal
of a vehicle are shown in Fig. 4.
Through extensive data analysis shows that, under normal
circumstances, the interference waveform’s change is rela-
tively ﬂat, although there will be the formation of stable drift,
but no occurrence of the waveform several times undulating.
Interference brought by the by-pass moving vehicles on the
road produces a short period of time ﬂuctuations, and thus
will not form a stable drift.
At present, the existing research results are mostly based
on the stable drift of parking stop phase to detect vehicle
occupancy, but the interference of neighbor parking will also
form similar drift, especially in the case of low signal-to-
noise ratio, as shown in Fig. 4. A vehicle presence detection
method is proposed based on the integrated magnetic signal
characteristics of a vehicle’s entering and parking stop phases.

C. Detection Algorithm
Occupancy of parking space is determined by a state
machine, as shown in Fig. 7. The state machine consists of:
State: {Init, Vacant, Fluct entering, Occupied, Fluct leaving}
Input [ F(i ),W (x), Leaving]: {0, 1}
Output [ D(i )]: {0, 1}
The Boolean ﬂags F(i ) is passed to the state machine. Their
main objective is to trace the ﬂuctuate signals that are caused
by vehicles entering or leaving. D(i ) is the output of the
detection state machine. Only when the state machine is in
“Occupied” or “ Fluct
leaving ”, D(i ) = 1, otherwise, D(i ) = 0.
The following section is a walk-through of the state machine’s
logical ﬂow.
S1 “Init”: Assuming there is no vehicle in the parking
space when the sensor node is being deployed. It will go into
state S1 and start initializing the background value with the
environmental magnetic ﬁeld.
S2 “Vacant”: After a pre-deﬁned initializing time, it will
jump to the state S2 where the arrays Pentering and Pleaving
are initialized. It will jump to state S3 there has an “ F(i ) = 1”
and trace the possible entering ﬂuctuation.
S3 “Fluctentering ”: It was found that a vehicle entering the
parking space produces a successive sequence of “ F(i ) = 1”
and this state is used to track such a sequence. The algorithm
extracts the peak values of the ﬂuctuation and restores them in
the array Pentering . If there is any “0” reading from F(i ),t h e
algorithm computes W (x).I f W (x)> = 0, it will immediately
jump to state S4. Otherwise, W (x)< 0, it will jump back to
state S2.
Fig. 8. Devices are deployed in roadside parking spaces. (a) Sensor nodes.
(b) Router.
S4 “Occupied”: Staying in this state implies the vehicle
is on the sensor node, and the parking space is occupied.
It initializes the array Pleaving . It will jump to S5 when the
sensor detected a possible leaving ﬂuctuation.
S5 “Fluct leaving ”: Within this state, it extracts the peak
values of the ﬂuctuation and restores them in the array
Pleaving . If there is any “0” reading from F(i ), the algorithm
computes the function Leaving. It will jump back to state S4
if Leaving = 0. Otherwise, Leaving = 1, it will jump to
state S2.

A. System Introduction
The sensor node has Zigbee [28] based communication
modules and it consists of HMC5883L [29] magnetic sensor.
The Zigbee wireless module is using the CC2530 MCU [30].
For battery powered sensor nodes, the lifetime is a critical
factor. The sensor node has n o speciﬁc responsibility for
maintaining the network infra structure because it is an end-
device [28]. So it doesn’t exchange packet for network main-
taining after joining the network. Sensor node samples the
magnetic signal periodically. When it detected a car arrival
or leave, it transmits one binary detection ﬂag to base station.
And it goes to sleep when no task to save energy. Using a pair
of 2500mAh AA Li batteries parallel connected, the sensor
node can continuously work for 5 years without changing
battery.

nodes in the roadside parking. As shown in Fig. 8 (a). Devices
with AMR sensors are nailed in the center of the parking

IV . CONCLUSION
In this paper, a parking occupancy detection algorithm based
on AMR sensor is presented. It focuses on characteristic of the
interference signal caused by adjacent vehicles, and proposes
a more accurate detection algor ithm than existing algorithms
both on the detections of vehicle parking and leaving. The
parking occupancy monitoring system, which includes eighty-
two sensor nodes, was deployed on a roadside parking. The
system has been running reliably for six months. Extensively
experiment results show that the system detection accuracy is
better than 98%.
